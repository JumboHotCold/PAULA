Objective:
Thepurpose of thispolicy is to ensure timely andresponsible online posting on St.Paul UniversitySurigao'sofficial online
platforms.The guidelines aim to maintain a professional,informative,and inclusive online presence that alignswith the
valuesandmissionoftheinstitution.
1.Authority and Responsibility: The Publications,Brand,and Marketing Office of St. Paul University Surigao is
responsible for overseeing and coordinating all online postings on the oficial institutional web page.
2. Timeliness:
2.1. All information posted on the school's official online pages should be relevant and timely.
2.2.Regular updates,announcements,and news items should be posted promptly,ideally,not later than 2
days,tokeepthecommunity informed.
2.3.Other contents like Audio-Visual Presentationsmay be released on a later date granted that apost
regarding the same activity hadalreadybeenposted.This is to give ample time forbroadcasting team and
editors to go through the tedious process of post-editing andpolishing of the material.
3. Content Criteria:
3.1. Information posted must align with the mission, vision, and values of St. Paul University Surigao.
3.2.Contentshould be accurate,reliable,and free from any form of misinformation.
3.3.Respect for diversity and inclusion must be observed in all postings.
4. Approval Process:
The University'sPublications,Brand,and Marketing Oficewill review and approve the final
contentbeforepublication.
5. Types of Activities to be Posted:
The following are the activities af school events that may beposted on the University's official social
media platforms:
5.1.Academic Achievements:Recognitions,awards,and notable accomplishments by students,faculty,and
staff.
5.2.Official Announcements: Important messages from the administration,policy changes,and official
communications.
5.3.Events: Information about upcoming institutional events, conferences, seminars,and workshops.
5.4.Community Outreach: Highlights of community service, social responsibility,and partnerships.
5.6.Student Activities:News about student-organizations,club.activities,and achievements.
6.Visual Content:
6.1. All visual content, including images and videos, must'adhere to copyright laws and respect the dignity of
individuals.
6.2. Graphic design and branding standards should be maintained consistenty across all posts.
7. Review and Revision: These guidelines will be periodically reviewed and revised as needed to adapt to changing
circumstances or technology.

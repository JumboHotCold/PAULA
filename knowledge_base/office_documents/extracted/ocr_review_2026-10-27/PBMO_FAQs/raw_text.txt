3. Guidelines for University-Related Announcements and Promotional Materials
·Q: How are announcements and promotional materials broadcast across the campus?
A: PBMO utilizes the Unified Multimedia Communication Network-comprising outdoor LED displays,
Campus TV monitors, social media channels, and the web portal—-to broadcast real-time news, institutional
announcements, emergency advisories, and updates from civic/government partners. All promotional assets,
announcements, and posters must undergo content and brand review by PBMO prior to broadcast or posting.
4.Branding and Proper Use of University Assets
. Q: What are the guidelines regarding official university logos, seals, and brand materials?
A: Official logo files, color palettes, and typography rules must be requested directly from PBMO. The official
university name, logo, seal, and related branding assets must not be modified, stretched, recolored, or
combined with unauthorized graphic elements. All materials meant for public consumption must adhere to
the official SPUS brand identity standards.
5. Requesting Documentation or Coverage of University Events
.Q: How can departments or student organizations request event photo/video coverage?
A: Submit a formal written or electronic request addressed to the PBMO Director at least three (3) to five (5)
working days before the scheduled event, outlining the event title, date, time, venue, and requested
deliverables. For official graduation pictorials and yearbook documentation, PBMO coordinates directly with
approved outsourced service providers under designated schedules.
6. General Guidelines for Submitting Information or Materials for Posting
.Q: How far in advance should materials be submitted for digital or portal posting?
A: Submissions for official social media posts or student portal updates should be turned in at least two (2)
working days prior to the target publishing date to allow adequate time for review, layout verification, and
scheduling.

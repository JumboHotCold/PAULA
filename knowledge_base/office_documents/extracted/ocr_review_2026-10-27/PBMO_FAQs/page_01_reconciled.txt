1.Publication and Documentation Services
.Q: What services are provided by the Publication, Brand, and Marketing Office (PBMO)?
A: PBMO is responsible for designing, editing, and publishing official university publications (e.g., 1st
Semester President's Report, The PauliGlobe student publication for Basic and Higher Education, and annual
Yearbooks). The office also oversees university branding, manages digital media pipelines (including official
sociai media platforms and the Unified Multimedia Communication Network), produces institutional
marketing/promotional materials, and documents major university events and heritage milestones.

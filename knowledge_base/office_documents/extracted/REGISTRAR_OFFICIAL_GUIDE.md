# St. Paul University Surigao (SPUS)
## University Registrar's Office — Official Enrollment & Academic Records Procedures

**Office Unit:** University Registrar's Office (URO)  
**Governance Scope:** Basic and Higher Education (College)  
**Document Classification:** Official Office Softcopy (Tier 1 Authority)

---

### 1. Changing, Adding, Dropping, or Withdrawal of Subjects (URO-PR-007)

**Objective:** Standardized guidelines for students seeking subject schedule or curricular modifications.  
**Scope:** Officially enrolled students in basic and higher education.

#### Procedure Steps:
1. **Form Request (Student):** Approaches the Registrar's Clerk and requests a Changing/Adding/Dropping/Withdrawal form.
2. **Form Issuance (Clerk):** Clerk issues the official numbered form.
3. **Form Accomplishment (Student):** Fills out the form completely with course codes, sections, reasons, and dates.
4. **Signatures & Approvals:** Student routes the form to secure formal approvals from:
   - College Dean / Program Chair
   - Subject Teacher / Subject Team Leader (STL)
   - University Registrar
   - Vice President for Finance Services
5. **Filing Copies (Student):** Submits final signed copies to:
   - Finance Office (for tuition/fee ledger adjustments)
   - University Registrar's Office (for official SIS encoding and record updates)

---

### 2. Enrollment Procedure for Old / Continuing Students (URO-PR-016)

**Scope:** All continuing students in Higher Education.

#### Procedure Steps:
1. **Academic Advising (Dean's Office):** Student presents curriculum checklist to Dean or Program Chair for advising. Dean's office prints the **Pre-Registration Form**.
2. **Assessment of Fees (Student Accounts / Finance):** Student takes the Pre-Registration Form to Student Accounts for computation of tuition, lab fees, and discounts. Student Accounts prints the **Assessment Form**.
3. **Payment (Cashier):** Student pays the required enrollment down payment or full tuition at the Cashier and receives the **Official Receipt (OR)**.
4. **Encoding & Validation (Registrar's Office):** Student submits the Official Receipt and Assessment to the Registrar's Clerk for system encoding, validation, and finalization of enrollment.

---

### 3. Enrollment Procedure for Transferee Students (URO-PR-017)

**Scope:** Transferee students entering Higher Education.

#### Required Documents:
- Official Transfer Credentials (Honorable Dismissal / Transcript of Records for evaluation)
- Curricular Checklist from previous school
- Original PSA Birth Certificate
- Original Certificate of Good Moral Character
- 1 pc. 2x2 ID picture
- Student Information Sheet

#### Procedure Steps:
1. **Dean's Office (Evaluation & Crediting):** Student presents Transfer Credentials and Curricular Checklist to Dean for transcript evaluation and subject crediting.
2. **Student Affairs Office (SAO):** Student fills out the Student Information Sheet and submits PSA Birth Certificate, Good Moral Certificate, and 2x2 picture. Data is encoded into the student system.
3. **Finance Office (Payment):** Student pays the initial or full enrollment fee at the Cashier/Finance Office, receiving the Pre-Registration Form and Official Receipt.
4. **Registrar's Office (Validation & Official Enrollment):** Student submits evaluated curriculum and Official Receipt to the Registrar's Office for validation. Receives official Study Load and Assessment stamped **"OFFICIALLY ENROLLED"**.
5. **ICT Office & Clinic:**
   - **ICT Office:** ID validation and processing.
   - **University Clinic:** Health Services medical profiling.

---

### 4. Enrollment Procedure for Incoming College Freshmen (URO-PR-005)

**Scope:** All incoming first-year college students.

#### Required Documents:
- Senior High School (SHS) Report Card (Form 138 / SF9)
- Original PSA Birth Certificate
- Original Certificate of Good Moral Character
- College Entrance Examination Result (from Guidance Office)
- 1 pc. 2x2 ID picture
- Marriage Certificate (if married)
- Accomplished Student Information Sheet

#### Step-by-Step Flow:
1. **Guidance Office & Admitting Officer:** Takes the entrance exam at the Guidance Office. Admitting officer verifies credentials and encodes student profile.
2. **Dean's Office:** Advising, schedule plotting, and section encoding. Receives Pre-Registration Form.
3. **Finance Office:** Assessment of tuition and fees, followed by payment at Cashier. Receives Official Receipt.
4. **Registrar's Office:** Submits Official Receipt and program curriculum for validation and verification. Receives official Study Load and Assessment stamped **"OFFICIALLY ENROLLED"**.
5. **ICT Office:** Secures the official student ID card by presenting the Official Receipt and stamped Study Load.
6. **University Clinic:** Fills out medical profiling form with the university nurse/physician.

### 1. Office Overview & Core Services
The Publication, Brand, and Marketing Office (PBMO) is the primary unit responsible for university-wide communications, brand stewardship, marketing productions, and institutional documentation:
- **Official Publications:** Monthly Institutional Newsletters, Semester President's Report, *The PauliGlobe* student publication (Basic and Higher Education), and annual Graduation Yearbooks.
- **Brand Management:** Oversight and governance of university branding, logos, seals, typography, and visual assets across all university units.
- **Digital Media Pipelines:** Management of official university social media channels, website content, and the **Unified Multimedia Communication Network** (outdoor campus LED displays, Campus TV monitors, and web portal).
- **Institutional Documentation:** High-definition photo and video documentation of major university events, celebrations, and heritage milestones.

### 2. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### Q1: What is the procedure for submitting materials or reporting school events for publication?
**Answer:** Submit the complete details of the article or event following the standard **5Ws and 1H framework** (*Who, What, When, Where, Why, and How*) along with high-resolution photos/media via email or soft copy directly to PBMO.  
*Mandatory Compliance Note:* Per institutional policy, all university employees attending school-funded seminars, conventions, or external trainings are strictly required to submit standard reporting details to PBMO for official publication, tracking, and inclusion in the President's Report.

#### Q2: How are announcements and promotional materials broadcast across campus?
**Answer:** PBMO broadcasts announcements through the **Unified Multimedia Communication Network** (outdoor LED displays, Campus TV monitors, social media channels, and the web portal). All promotional assets, announcements, and posters must undergo content and brand review by PBMO prior to broadcast or campus display.

#### Q3: What are the guidelines for official university logos, seals, and branding assets?
**Answer:** Official logo files, color palettes, and typography specifications must be requested directly from PBMO. The official university name, logo, seal, and branding assets must **never** be modified, stretched, recolored, or combined with unauthorized graphic elements. For legibility, keep the area around the logo clear. The minimum width for the logo is 0.5 inches.

#### Q4: How do departments or student organizations request photo or video event coverage?
**Answer:** Submit a formal written or electronic request addressed to the PBMO Director at least **three (3) to five (5) working days** before the scheduled event, indicating the event title, date, time, venue, and requested deliverables. For official graduation pictorials and yearbook documentation, PBMO coordinates directly with approved outsourced service providers under designated schedules.

#### Q5: How far in advance should materials be submitted for digital or student portal posting?
**Answer:** Submissions for official social media posts or student portal updates must be submitted at least **two (2) working days** prior to the target publishing date to allow adequate time for review, layout verification, and scheduling.

#### Q6: What are the office operating hours?
**Answer:**
- **Monday to Friday:** 8:00 AM – 12:00 NN | 1:30 PM – 5:00 PM
- **Saturday:** 8:00 AM – 12:00 PM (Noon)
- **Sunday & Institutional Holidays:** Closed

### 3. Policy on Online Posting (Document PBMO-PO-010 / PBMO-PR-009)

#### Objectives & Scope
Ensures timely, responsible, accurate, and mission-aligned online posting on all official SPUS digital channels. Applies to all academic and administrative units.

#### Key Principles:
1. **Authority:** PBMO oversees and coordinates all online postings on the official institutional webpage and social media channels.
2. **Timeliness:** Regular updates, announcements, and news items should be posted promptly—ideally not later than 2 days after the activity. Audio-Visual Presentations (AVPs) may be published later provided an initial event post has already been made.
3. **Content Standards:** Must align with Gospel values and SPUS vision/mission, remain accurate and free of misinformation, and respect diversity and inclusion.
4. **Approved Posting Categories:**
   - Academic Achievements (awards, recognitions of students, faculty, and staff)
   - Official Administrative Announcements (policies, institutional communications)
   - University Events (conferences, seminars, workshops, cultural celebrations)
   - Community Outreach (social responsibility, environmental initiatives, partnerships)
   - Student Activities (news and milestones from clubs and student organizations)

#### 4-Step Standard Procedure (PBMO-PR-009):
1. **Unit Personnel:** Submits request for documentation or laying out of publication materials (verbal, letter, email, text).
2. **Web, Layout & Marketing Staff:** Prepares publication layout, graphic design, and copy draft.
3. **PBMO Director:** Reviews and approves the final publication material.
4. **PBMO Director & Page Administrator:** Publishes the material to official online platforms.

### 4. University Brand Identity Standards (PBMO SPUS BRANDING)

#### University Seal & Identity:
- **Motto:** *Caritas* (Charity), *Veritas* (Truth), *Scientia* (Science/Knowledge)
- **Patron:** St. Paul the Apostle | **Model:** Blessed Virgin Mary
- **Heritage:** Founded 1906, Surigao City; under the charism of the Sisters of St. Paul of Chartres (SPC).

#### Official Color Palette:
- **Primary Colors:**
  - Paulinian Green: `#005301`, `#002c04`, `#215a2e`
  - Paulinian Gold / Yellow: `#deb402`, `#fcd108`
  *(Used for logos, official documents, letterheads, certificates, diplomas, banners, and signage)*
- **Secondary Colors:**
  - Deep Blue: `#0101c1`
  - Accent Red: `#da0200`
  *(Used solely to complement the primary palette; never to replace)*

#### Typography Guidelines:
- **Primary Font:** Reserved for headlines, titles, and key messages.
- **Secondary Font:** For body text, captions, and supporting content.
- **Strict Rule:** Never stretch, compress, or warp text. Avoid mixing unapproved fonts.
